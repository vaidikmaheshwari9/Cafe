The Choupal Story

Rooted in the Soul of Community & Malwa Warmth

In traditional Indian towns and villages, the Choupal is the community's heart: a shaded circle under an ancient tree where villagers assemble to share laughter, deliberate, narrate tales, and sip warm tea.

Located right here in Khandwa, Madhya Pradesh, Choupal Café was born from a passion to revive this leisurely, heartfelt connection in a modern world. We blend the vibrant, hospitable culinary traditions of the Nimar and Malwa regions with artisanal beverage brewing.

Sourced from Local Malwa Farms

Our ginger, cardamom, fresh dairy, and flour are handpicked from regional growers around Nimar and Indore.

An Inclusive Living Room for Khandwa

Whether you need a quiet corner with a book, high-speed Wi-Fi for remote work, or evening live music, our doors are open.

Slow Food & Conscious Hospitality

Served in eco-friendly terracotta kulhads and brassware, honoring our earth and ancestral craft traditions.
